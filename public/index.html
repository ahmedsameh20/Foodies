How it works

1. Choose & customise

Pick a restaurant, add dishes and extras to your cart. Prices are always confirmed by the restaurant's menu, never by your browser.

2. Pay your way

Pay by card on the payment provider's secure page (we never see your card), or choose cash on delivery.

3. Track your order

Follow the status from accepted to out for delivery. Need help? Message us on WhatsApp or report a problem from your order.

Run a restaurant?

Create your menu, receive orders in a live dashboard, and get paid every week. There is no monthly fee: the platform takes a commission of a small percentage on each order, shown transparently in your earnings. New restaurants are reviewed before they go live.
